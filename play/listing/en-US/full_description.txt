Bravin is a full-featured video editor for Android phones and tablets, built for vertical TikTok, Reels and Shorts as well as landscape YouTube video.

DESKTOP-CLASS EDITING
• Multi-track timeline: overlays, text, stickers, audio; ripple, roll, slip, slide, speed ramps
• Keyframes for position, scale, rotation, opacity and volume with smooth easing
• Ellipse, rectangle and bezier masks with motion tracking; chroma key and on-device AI person cut-out
• Clip groups, compound clips, markers, Premiere-style shortcuts with a keyboard

COLOR, EFFECTS, TEXT
• Lumetri-style color, .cube LUTs, DaVinci-style node color graph, scopes
• A large library of effects and transitions, denoise, stabilization, smooth slow motion
• Titles, captions and lyrics; nearly 2,000 Google Fonts installable in one tap

AUDIO AND AI
• On-device speech-to-text captions, text-to-speech
• −14 LUFS normalization, noise reduction, ducking, waveform audio sync
• AI assistant edits your timeline from plain-language commands using your own API key (OpenAI, Claude, Gemini, DeepSeek, Groq…)

S PEN AND LARGE SCREENS
• Pressure-sensitive pen drawing with eraser and palm rejection
• Hover the pen to preview frames, side button opens menus
• Galaxy Tab, Fold, Samsung DeX and TV casting switch to the full desktop layout with mouse and keyboard

EXPORT
• MP4 up to 4K, 24–60 fps, loudness normalized for social media
• SRT and FCP XML export; projects auto-save on device

PRIVACY
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your projects and videos stay on your device.

Plugins and the Micron add-on store are available in Bravin for desktop (Windows and web).
